Tickets & fares

City buses

Fare
40 lekë per ride
Payment
Cash only, paid on the bus. An attendant in an orange vest walks down the aisle and collects fares.
Operation
Usually daily from 6:00 to 23:00
Frequency
Every 3 to 13 minutes depending on the line
Lines
Numbered and lettered lines (such as 1A, 2, 3, 5, 9, 11 and the Unaza inner/outer ring) connect Skanderbeg Square and central hubs with neighbourhoods like Kombinat, Kinostudio, Lapraka and Qyteti Studenti.

Airport & suburban connections

TIA airport ⇄ Tirana centre

Rinas Express: a bus between Skanderbeg Square (behind the Opera and Ballet Palace) and Tirana International Airport (TIA).

Price on board
400 L or €4.50, in cash or by card (POS) directly on the bus, no extra fee
Schedule
Operates 24/7, every full hour

TIA airport ⇄ Jug–Sever terminal

LU-NA: a bus between the Jug–Sever regional bus terminal (departures to other Albanian cities) and Tirana International Airport (TIA).

Price on board
300 L or €3.50, in cash or by card on the bus
Schedule
Daily 7:00–18:00 (terminal to airport) and 8:00–18:00 (airport to terminal)

Intercity buses & terminals

The North/South bus terminal lies further out and serves regional and long-distance domestic routes (e.g. to Berat, Vlorë or Shkodër). Intercity departures can be looked up on the national platform eTransport.
